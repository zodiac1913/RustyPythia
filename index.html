About External Additional App

This launcher is secondary; the SQL workspace above is the main surface. Use it to open a completely separate SQL app in its own window, which is handy when you want a second tool side by side with Rusty Pythia.

Enter the app's full http:// or https:// URL, then pick Open in Tauri Window for a native window or Open in Browser for your default browser. The URL stands on its own, though a saved connection can optionally fill it in for you.
